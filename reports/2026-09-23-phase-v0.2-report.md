## Migrations

- `migrations/0003_phase_v0_2.sql`:
  1. Creates `plans` table with `account_id`, `pack_id`, `title`, `content`, `source_plan_id`, `visibility`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `archived_at`.
  2. Adds `plan_id` foreign key column to `sessions` table.
  3. Creates `notes` table with `account_id`, `session_id`, `group_id`, `learner_id`, `body`, `visibility`, `created_at`, `updated_at`.
  4. Creates required indexes:
     - `idx_plans_account_updated` ON `plans(account_id, updated_at DESC)`
     - `idx_plans_pack` ON `plans(account_id, pack_id)`
     - `idx_sessions_plan` ON `sessions(plan_id)`
     - `idx_notes_account_created` ON `notes(account_id, created_at DESC)`
     - `idx_notes_session` ON `notes(session_id)`
     - `idx_notes_group` ON `notes(group_id)`
     - `idx_notes_learner` ON `notes(learner_id)`
