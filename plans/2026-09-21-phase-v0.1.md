# Phase v0.1 Plan: Core Planner (Workplaces, Groups, Schedule, Attendance)

## Goal
Implement the core planner functionality of ClassQue, encompassing Workplaces and Terms, Groups and Learners, Schedule and Sessions (Today/Week views), and Attendance. This phase establishes the primary load-bearing invariants of the application, such as session generation, idempotent top-ups, and attendance rate calculations. 

## Requirement IDs in Scope
- **WPL-001 to WPL-006, WPL-008** (Workplaces and Terms; WPL-007 deferred)
- **GRP-001 to GRP-006** (Groups)
- **LRN-001 to LRN-008** (Learners and Memberships)
- **SCH-001 to SCH-008** (Schedule and Sessions)
- **TDY-001 to TDY-008, TDY-010, TDY-011** (Today and Week views)
- **ATT-001 to ATT-010** (Attendance)
- **DM-010, DM-011, DM-012, DM-013, DM-020 to DM-025** (Domain Invariants)

## Risks
- **D1 Free Limits**: Heavy `top-up` generation might read/write too many rows if not strictly batched and limited. MUST use `db.batch` for all session generation and attendance saving.
- **Timezone Math**: Validating `session_date` and `start_time` across IANA timezones (SCH-008, TDY-002) is complex. Must strictly treat dates as local wall-clock time strings until exact comparison is needed.
- **Complex UI State**: Unsaved drafts for attendance (ATT-005) conflicting with React Query refetches.

## Assumptions Made
- The user is building on the corrected Phase 0 state where `.dev.vars` and gitignore are fixed, and blocking revisions are merged.
- `Intl.Collator` will be used exclusively on the frontend for Learner sorting (LRN-007) since SQLite doesn't natively support full localized collation without extensions.
- All session generation math assumes max 56 days into the future (DM-020) per the spec.
- For ATT-002 ("Mark all present"), explicit marks are never overwritten. A learner previously marked `excused` in a draft will not be changed to `present` by "Mark all present".
